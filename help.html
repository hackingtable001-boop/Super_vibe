Popular articles

Create a board from a template, add your first tasks, and invite teammates — all from the "New board" button in your workspace.

Go to Settings → Import, upload a CSV, and map your columns to task fields. Most imports finish in under a minute.

Open a board's Automations tab, choose a trigger (like "task moved to Done") and an action (like "notify assignee"), then save.

Go to Settings → Billing to upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and yearly billing at any time.

From a board's Share menu, choose "Invite guest" and enter their email — guests can comment without using a full seat.
