Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 — Demo content for illustration only.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account info (name, email)To create and secure your account
Billing detailsTo process payments (handled by our payment processor)
Workspace contentTo provide the boards, tasks and files you create
Usage dataTo fix bugs and improve the product

How we use it

We use your data to operate super_vibe, respond to support requests, and send essential account emails. Marketing emails are opt-in and every message includes an unsubscribe link.

Sharing

We don't sell personal data. We share data only with service providers who help us run super_vibe (hosting, email delivery, payments), under agreements that limit how they can use it.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in, and optional analytics cookies to understand product usage. You can decline analytics cookies from the banner shown on your first visit.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or request deletion at any time by contacting privacy@supervibe.app.

Data retention

If you cancel your account, your data remains available in read-only mode for 90 days so you can export it, then it's permanently deleted.
