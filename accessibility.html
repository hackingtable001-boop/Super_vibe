Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 1, 2026 — Demo content for illustration only.

We want super_vibe to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology like screen readers, screen magnifiers, or keyboard-only navigation.

What we aim for

  • Meeting WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines across the product
  • Full keyboard navigation for core workflows (boards, tasks, comments)
  • Sufficient color contrast, including in dark mode
  • Descriptive alt text for meaningful images
  • Clear focus states on interactive elements

Known limitations

We're aware some parts of the product — particularly drag-and-drop board interactions — are still being improved for screen reader and keyboard-only users. An alternative list view is available for these workflows in the meantime.

Feedback

If you run into an accessibility barrier anywhere in super_vibe, please let us know at accessibility@supervibe.app or through our contact page. We aim to respond within two business days.
