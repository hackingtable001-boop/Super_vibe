How Studio Flux cut meeting time by 40%

A 14-person design studio replaced five tools with one shared board — and got its Fridays back.

−40%Time in status meetings
3 weeksFull team onboarding
5 → 1Tools replaced with one board

The problem

Studio Flux ran client work across a task tracker, a shared drive, email threads and a separate spreadsheet just to report status. Creative director Marina Boyko estimates the team spent close to a day a week just keeping those tools in sync.

"We weren't slow because the work was hard. We were slow because everything lived in a different place."

What changed

The studio moved all active projects onto super_vibe over a single two-week sprint. Briefs, comments and deadlines moved onto shared boards, and the team's daily status call was replaced by an async check-in built into the board itself.

The result

Within a month, the studio had cut its weekly meeting load nearly in half and reported that client feedback turned around noticeably faster, since comments landed directly on the relevant file instead of getting summarized secondhand in a status email.
