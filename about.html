We build calmer tools for teams who care about the work

super_vibe started as a side project for one overworked design studio. Today it helps thousands of teams plan without the chaos.

How it started

Our founders ran a small design studio and spent more time managing spreadsheets than doing actual design work. Every new client meant a new folder, a new tracker, a new way to lose track of feedback.

super_vibe began as an internal tool to fix that — a single board that held briefs, comments and deadlines together. When other studios asked to use it too, we turned it into a product.

Four years later, the mission hasn't changed: give creative and product teams one calm place to work, instead of ten disconnected tools.

What we care about

A short list of the things that actually shape how we build the product.

Calm over clever

We'd rather ship something simple that teams actually use than a feature that looks impressive in a demo.

Fewer meetings, better ones

If a feature doesn't reduce the need for a status call, we ask whether it belongs in the product at all.

Built by people who use it

Our own team runs entirely on super_vibe — every rough edge we ship, we feel first.
